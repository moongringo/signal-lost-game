WebGL Shader Library

Production-ready GLSL shaders for high-fidelity websites. Based on top 200 Awwwards winners.

🎞️ Film Grain

Organic film grain with animated noise. Used by 60% of award sites for texture. grain = fract(sin(dot(uv, vec2(12.9898,78.233))) * 43758.5453);

🌊 Liquid Distortion

Mouse-reactive fluid distortion. Perfect for hero sections. distortion = sin(uv.y * 10.0 + time) * 0.02;

✨ Kinetic Gradient

Animated mesh gradient with organic movement. 2025 trend. color = mix(c1, c2, sin(time + uv.x * 3.14) * 0.5 + 0.5);

🔦 Vignette Spotlight

Mouse-tracking radial spotlight with vignette. Atmospheric depth. vignette = 1.0 - smoothstep(0.3, 0.8, length(uv - mouse));
