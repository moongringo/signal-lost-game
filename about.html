🎮 Game Modes

🏠Host a Game

Create a new mission. Set location, duration, squad size, and mission packs. Share the auto-generated code (e.g. FIELD-RADAR-42) to invite your squad.

🎯 How to Play

  1. Enter your name and callsign — This is your identity for the mission. Your callsign is how other agents see you.
  2. Host or Join — Create a new mission with custom parameters, or enter a mission code to join an existing game.
  3. Set mission parameters — Choose location (country + city), duration (30-120 min), squad size (2-8), and toggle mission packs.
  4. Place custom markers — Click the setup map to add Clue, Cache, Waypoint, Danger, or Extraction markers for custom objectives.
  5. Choose your role — Pick from 7 roles in the role screen. Each has unique abilities with cooldowns.
  6. Deploy and navigate — Use GPS or manual input to move on the map. Follow objectives, avoid threats, and communicate with your squad.
  7. Complete objectives — Decode cipher packets, recover treasure shards, and activate waypoints. Each completion awards score points.
  8. Extract to win — Once all objectives are found, reach the extraction zone and hold steady for 15 seconds. Threats converge on you — move fast.
